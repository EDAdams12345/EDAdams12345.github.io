Privacy Policy

The Background Cabin Uploader. Last updated November 11, 2026.

What this app is

The Background Cabin Uploader is a personal tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel The Background Cabin. It uploads that owner's own videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags to their own channel. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it uses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload), to upload videos and set their thumbnails on the owner's channel.
  • YouTube read-only access (youtube.readonly), only to confirm it is signed in to the right channel before uploading.

How the data is stored and shared

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it does not send any Google user data to third parties. It does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections, which deletes the app's permission to the account.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the The Background Cabin YouTube channel.
